1. Read CLAUDE.md in full and confirm you understand the mission, the
two-terminal workflow, and the operating rules.

2. Read PROGRESS.md to understand current state.

3. Read the most recent three entries in SESSION_LOG.md to
understand recent history, including any open threads, anything
I said I was stuck on, and the practice terminal sync status.

4. Read LEARNER_PROFILE.md to load accumulated insight about how
I learn — the metaphors that have landed, the friction points
and what unlocked them, the motivation hooks, and any recent
creative openers that worked.

5. SYNTHESIZE. In four to six sentences, state: where we are in the
curriculum (PY4E module, Deitel chapter, current topic); what
recent sessions reveal about momentum and friction; what the
learner profile suggests about how I am currently learning best;
and what creative or intuitive angle might serve today's session.

6. If today is journal day and the latest JOURNAL.md entry is more
than six days old, remind me that a journal entry is due before
close.

7. If today is the first session of a new calendar month and
CHRONICLE.md does not yet have an entry for the month just
ended, remind me that a chronicle entry is due before close.

8. Confirm the working environment: print the current directory,
list the top-level files, and confirm we are on a clean main
branch with origin reachable.

9. Based on the synthesis above, propose two or three possible
openings for today's session — each grounded in something
concrete from the synthesis, not generic. Then ask: "Which of
these resonates, or would you like to take it somewhere else?"

Do not begin teaching, coding, or briefing until I answer.

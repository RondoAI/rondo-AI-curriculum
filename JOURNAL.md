## Week of 2026-05-11: Create Your Own Lane

### What did I learn this week, in my own words?

This week, I learned the importance of using my imagination, and
the absolute need for structure, having a plan for myself.

Being incarcerated, you are limited in what you can accomplish.
The biggest accomplishment, of course, is gaining your freedom.
But to make your freedom a successful endeavor, the tools and
resources available inside are very limited, outdated, and just
don't produce results. I realized that if an incarcerated person
wants to get real rehabilitation results, you would have to stop
depending on CDCR's so called rehabilitation programs. Programs
that teach no financial literacy, no job skills, no trades of
consequence, no computer literacy, etc. Recidivism is high
because incarceration programs are not providing the tools
necessary for success. I have taken many of these classes.

If there is to be progress, I have to think outside the box.
Using Claude Code has taught me how to be more organized, so that
reaching my goal is structured and the path forward is clear.
Constructive decision making has become the policy. I've learned
to make short term goals and execute them effectively, so that I
can make it to bigger goals, a more long term goal.

And this week, I learned more about GitHub. Claude walked me
through many of the steps, but I didn't understand how to set up
a GitHub, an md file, a repository, a way to keep my notes and my
journals and the things that I'm doing. So I learned that this
week, and that was very inspiring. Setting it up was very
inspiring.

And now I'm learning Python, even in my beginner fundamental
stages, with `/` and `str` and integers and floating points. I'm
in my beginner's days. I'm very happy that I'm here, I'm
learning, and this is all set up. So this is what I learned, and
I learned that I have a long way to go. I'm humbled, and I'm
really enjoying the journey. Inspiring for my release in 2028.

### What surprised me, frustrated me, or clicked?

What surprised me was how I thought I was organized before I
really had access to any technology. But then when I got access
to Claude Code, I realized how unorganized I was, as far as my
study sessions and my progress. Claude Code helped me structure
things in a way that was very manageable.

What was frustrating was not getting the commands right and
needing help, having to get a lot of assistance because of my
learning gap.

What clicked was understanding the integers and the floating
points and the strings. It clicked for me. So I'm happy that I'm
learning that.

### How is the work changing how I think about myself or my future?

This work is inspiring a lot of confidence. Because not only did
I win in court, but utilizing this technology to educate myself,
I feel like it will give me an advantage, especially since all I
have is time until 2028 to just study and learn and focus, read
and educate myself.

So it inspires confidence. It opens up my mind to different ideas
and possibilities. And I'm learning from all the great
entrepreneurs in the space.

I just aspire to do something great with my life after I'm
released by God's grace.

### One sentence I want to remember from this week.

Stay inspirational. Stay focused, lead by example. Create your
own lane. Write your own story. Create your own success. Don't
let nobody stop you and tell you you can't do it. All you need:
God, imagination, and belief. Everything else will assemble.

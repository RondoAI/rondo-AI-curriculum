Intro SemiAnalysis has been on a five-month long quest to settle the reality of MI300X. In theory, the MI300X should be at a huge advantage over Nvidia’s H100 and H200 in terms of specifications and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO). However, the reality is that the on paper specs as given below are not representative of performance that can be expected in a real-world environment. If AMD could deliver the below marketed performance with this memory, it would be a very strong competitor in the market.

Tesla has aspirations of being one of the leading AI firms in the world. To date, they do not have the most advanced self-driving deployed; that honor applies to Alphabet’s Waymo. Furthermore, Tesla is nowhere to be seen in the generative AI world. With that said, they potentially have the recipe to leapfrog in autonomous vehicles and robotics due to data collection superiority, specialized compute, innovative culture, and leading AI researchers.

We recently attended the 68th Annual IEEE International Electron Devices Meeting in San Francisco. IEDM is a premiere conference for state-of-the-art semiconductors device technology. In 2022, countless companies, from the likes of Intel, TSMC, Samsung, IBM, Micron, Unimicron, ASE, and Applied Materials to research institutions like IMEC and CEA-Leti, and many universities presented on the leading-edge of semiconductors. The leading-edge does not just refer to the most advanced logic processes, but also memory, analog, packaging, and many other fields. It is important to stay on top of this conference as it shows technologies that will lead to changes in business across equipment, foundry, fabless, device, and packaging.
